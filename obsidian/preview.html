Autumn rail trip

Four days along the coast by regional train. No car, one bag each, and a rule that every leg ends somewhere with a bakery. Gear lives in Packing list, fares come from the regional timetable, and the ferry leg still waits on Ferry times.

#travel#slow-trip

Route

Day one — harbour town

Morning train out, lunch on the pier, then the cliff path while the light holds. The sleeper leaves at 22:40 from platform 3b.

Book the sleeper early
Couchettes sell out weeks ahead on autumn weekends. Ask for a lower berth on the window side.
